About the Game

Platform Zero is an interactive mystery game. The player wakes up alone in an underground station with only ten minutes left before the station shuts down.

Every decision takes time. The player must explore the station, find clues and decide who to trust.

The game will have different endings depending on the player's choices. We are planning a simple PS1-inspired visual style.
